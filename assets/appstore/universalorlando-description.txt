Skip the guesswork. UOR Queues brings live Universal Orlando Resort data straight to your Pebble — no phone-fumbling, no app-switching, no squinting at a tiny map.

What you get:
- Live wait times for every attraction across Universal Studios, Islands of Adventure, Epic Universe, and Volcano Bay.
- Show schedules for every performance, with the next start time highlighted.
- Opening and closing hours for each park, shown right on the park picker so you know at a glance where's still open.
- Favourites — long-press any ride to pin it to the top of the list, kept in sync across park switches.
- Launcher glance: park hours count down live in your app launcher, up to a week ahead, without even opening the app.
- Sort by wait (ascending or descending) or alphabetically.

Built for the watch:
- Readable at a glance. Traffic-light colour coding (green / amber / red) means you don't need to read numbers to know if it's a walk-on or a two-hour queue.
- Times follow your watch's 12/24-hour clock setting.
- Works on both Pebble Time 2 (rectangular) and Pebble Round 2 (circular) — with native layouts for each.
- Auto-refreshes every five minutes while open, without ever losing your place; pull the latest on demand from settings.

Powered by ThemeParks.wiki — the community-maintained open API for theme-park wait times.
