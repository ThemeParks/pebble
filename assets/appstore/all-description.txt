Skip the guesswork. ThemePark Queues brings live theme-park data straight to your Pebble — covering dozens of parks across four continents. No phone-fumbling, no app-switching, no squinting at a tiny map.

Pick a destination:
- Walt Disney World, Disneyland Paris, Tokyo Disneyland, Hong Kong Disneyland, Shanghai Disneyland.
- Universal Orlando, Universal Studios Hollywood, Universal Studios Japan.
- Plus Six Flags, Cedar Fair, Merlin parks, and more — anywhere ThemeParks.wiki covers.

What you get:
- Live wait times for every attraction in the parks we cover.
- Show schedules with the next start time highlighted.
- Opening and closing hours for each park, shown right on the park picker so you know at a glance where's still open.
- Favourites — pin your must-do rides and shows to the top of the list, kept in sync across park switches.
- Sort by wait (ascending or descending) or alphabetically.

Built for the watch:
- Readable at a glance. Traffic-light colour coding (green / amber / red) means you don't need to read numbers to know if it's a walk-on or a two-hour queue.
- Works on both Pebble Time 2 (rectangular) and Pebble Round 2 (circular) — with native layouts for each.
- Auto-refreshes every five minutes while open; pull the latest on-demand by switching parks.

Location-aware: ThemePark Queues uses your phone's GPS to nudge nearby destinations to the top of the list, so you're one tap from live waits the moment you walk through the gate.

Powered by ThemeParks.wiki — the community-maintained open API for theme-park wait times.
